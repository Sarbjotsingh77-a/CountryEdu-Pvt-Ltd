Got Questions? We've Got Answers

Find answers to the most common questions about our programs, certifications, mentorship and career support.

Are these programs suitable for beginners?

Yes. Our programs are designed for absolute beginners as well as working professionals.

Will I receive a certificate after completion?

Yes, every learner receives an industry recognized certificate after successfully completing the program.

Do you provide internship opportunities?

Yes. Selected programs include internship assistance and placement support.

Is there any live mentorship?

Yes. You will get access to live doubt sessions, 1:1 mentorship and expert guidance.

Do I need any prior coding experience to join these programs?

Not at all. Most of our programs are designed for beginners as well as working professionals. We start with the fundamentals and gradually move to advanced concepts through hands-on projects and practical assignments.

Will I get real-world projects and a certificate after completing the program?

Yes. Every program includes industry-oriented projects that help you build a strong portfolio. Upon successful completion, you'll also receive a certificate that showcases your skills and achievements.
