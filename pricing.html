Frequently Asked Questions

Pricing & Billing FAQ

Have questions about enrollment, payment methods, or course access? Find your answers below.

Is there any hidden fee in these pricing plans?

No, there are zero hidden fees. The price you see is all-inclusive and grants complete access to videos, project files, and certificates.

Can I upgrade from a Single Program to a Combo Package later?

Yes! You can easily upgrade your learning track at any time by paying the difference through your LMS dashboard.

What payment options are supported?

We support all major payment methods including UPI, Credit Cards, Debit Cards, Net Banking, and EMI options.

What happens after I purchase a program?

Once your payment is successfully completed, you’ll receive access to your enrolled program and learning dashboard. You can start with the structured curriculum, access learning resources, complete assignments and projects, and track your progress throughout the program.

Can I access the program from my mobile or tablet?

Yes. Our programs are designed to be accessible across mobile phones, tablets, laptops, and desktops. You can learn at your convenience as long as you have a stable internet connection.

Do I get lifetime access to the course materials?

Yes, all enrolled students enjoy lifetime access to course contents, resources, and future curriculum updates.
